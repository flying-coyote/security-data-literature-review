## 3. Positioning

The project's dual identity — living evidence backbone for the SDW program, and a PRISMA-two-arm manuscript for an academic venue — is stronger than the current framing admits, because the best-practices research came back with named methodological homes for both halves:

**Name the design.** What this review is, in the published-guidelines vocabulary, is a **Multivocal Literature Review** (Garousi, Felderer & Mäntylä, IST 2019) run as a **living systematic review** (Elliott et al., J Clin Epi 2017; Cochrane LSR guidance 2019). That naming matters: Garousi's Table-4 decision aid says grey literature is *required*, not tolerated, when practitioner knowledge isn't in the academic record and context shapes outcomes — this topic answers yes on at least four of their seven questions — so the ~60% grey share stops being a Level-A shortfall to apologize for and becomes a compliant design decision to cite. The 2026-07-10 vendor-channel authorship rule turns out to be Garousi's expertise-dimension model applied correctly, and the A-D tiers map onto their Table-7 criteria. None of this requires new work, only claiming in §2 what the repo already practices.

**The honesty stance is evidence-backed, but the framing rule is specific.** Lu et al. (Scientific Reports 2013) found the citation penalty to an author's prior work follows *non-self-reported* retractions, while self-reporting authors showed no spillover penalty, and Azoulay et al. (2017) found the career penalty concentrates on misconduct rather than honest error. So the manuscript should state plainly that the audit was self-initiated — nobody external caught the fabrications — because self-initiation is the variable the literature says converts correction from damage into signal. The tone rule from the precedents (Rohrer et al.'s Loss-of-Confidence Project 2021 as the positive model, Reinhart-Rogoff 2013 as the cautionary one): lead with what was wrong and what was withdrawn, then give the re-measured numbers as the proof the conclusions survive; never lead with "the central conclusions stand." COPE's correction vocabulary and NISO RP-45-2024 (CREC) give the WITHDRAWN-strike convention a standards citation that costs one sentence and reads as method rather than confession.

**But the manuscript currently over-rotates into self-indictment.** The abstract is 426 words against JCS's 350-word citation-free cap, and roughly 40% of it is audit mechanics; the withdrawal narrative repeats at ten-plus sites (lines 309, 524, 536, 562, 572, 630, 648-656, 764, plus table footnotes); and aphoristic asides ("the review's own thesis turned back on itself", "sharpest where it hurts most", "The peer-reviewed base of this review is younger than the review") editorialize against the work. The measured facts — zero recall, the venue appraisal, the author-dependency finding — are the paper's most publishable methodological contributions. State each once, in neutral register, and let them carry.

**Venue: the ruling holds, with numbers corrected.** Journal of Cybersecurity remains right: research papers run to 15,000 words (the ~10k manuscript fits with headroom for reviewer additions; Perspectives cap at 5,000 so submit as research), the journal has published PRISMA SLRs (tyad023 2024; tyae026 2024, explicitly PRISMA; tyag004 2026, Kitchenham-method) to cite as in-venue precedent, references are already the right numbered-bracket style, preprints are explicitly permitted (arXiv cs.CR first is safe), and the data-availability policy makes the DOI'd repo a strength. Corrections to the venue doc: the APC is **$3,625 USD**, not the ~£1,500-2,000 at PUBLICATION-VENUE-RECOMMENDATIONS.md:122 (roughly 2× understated; the low figures circulating are stale) — as an unfunded independent, file OUP's discretionary waiver **with the submission** (decision ~5 working days, hidden from reviewers), and pin the exact APC at submission per the existing deferred item. ORCID is required at submission and currently absent from every metadata surface. Computers & Security stays the named backup (tighter topical fit, zero-cost subscription route, but more conventional reviewers who will press the grey-lit arm harder). The F1000Research versioned model (Schmidt et al. 2021, 10:401, is a published living SLR) does not reopen the ruled venue question — its value is as the named precedent for the quarterly-version mechanics and the citation-with-version convention.

**The unaffiliated-practitioner credibility mechanics** are three concrete artifacts, all cheap: an ORCID iD in the author block ("Independent Researcher, Security Data Works"), the Zenodo-DOI'd corpus cited from the availability statements, and a retrospective **OSF registration** of the living-review protocol (PROSPERO is ineligible — health outcomes only) carrying a dated status-at-registration section. The git history and quarterly tags cryptographically timestamp what existed when, which turns the retrofit into an auditable provenance trail; disclosed retrospective registration is the norm-compliant honest move, and it upgrades PRISMA item 24a from "not registered" to a DOI.
